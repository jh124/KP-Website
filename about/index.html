Kidderminster Press

Est. 2026

Kidderminster Press was founded in 2026 with the aim of bringing neglected works of Christian theology back into print. Our particular interest is in the writings of the English Presbyterians, though our catalogue is by no means limited to them.

Many of the finest works in the history of Christian theology have long been out of print or remain difficult to obtain. We believe that much of this literature deserves a far wider readership than it currently enjoys.

Our chief aim is not commercial success, but the recovery and circulation of these neglected works. We hope both to make them available in print for those already acquainted with them and to introduce them to a new generation of readers.
