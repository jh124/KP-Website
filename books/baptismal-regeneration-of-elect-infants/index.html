About this edition

First published in 1629, Baptismal Regeneration of Elect Infants by Cornelius Burges (1589-1665) gives a thorough defense of the efficacy of the sacrament of Baptism as it relates to the children of believers. Burges defends the thesis that elect infants ordinarily receive the Holy Spirit in Baptism, implanting in them the principal of spiritual life that, in God's own time, will bear fruit in faith and repentance. Drawing Extensively from Scripture, Church Fathers, Reformed Confessions, and the writings of eminent Reformed Divines such as Martin Bucer and Peter Martyr Vermigli, Burges presents a thoroughgoing defense of baptismal efficacy and the status of infants in Christ's Church.
