About this edition

The full text of 10 Reformed confessional documents, arranged topically, with every word from each confession appearing at least once. The confessions featured: Augsburg Confession, French Confession of Faith, Scots Confession, Belgic Confession, Heidelberg Catechism, Second Helvetic Confession, Thirty-Nine Articles, Canons of Dort, Westminster Confession of Faith, Westminster Larger Catechism.
