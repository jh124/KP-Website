The book

About this edition

This volume brings together two important works of James Hadow (1667-1747), A Scottish Minister, Professor at St. Mary's College, and leading opponent of Marrow theology. The first, The Record of God and the Duty of Faith, is a sermon on 1 John 5:11-12 delivered by Hadow which examines the nature of saving faith and the Gospel's call to repent and believe in Christ. In the second, The Antinomianism of the Marrow of Modern Divinity, Hadow delivers a substantive and robust critique of E. Fisher's (1627) classic work; demonstrating that it is not freed from the charge of Antinomianism.
